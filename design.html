Tally — design

Logo, notifications and widgets for the habit app, in the Apple Liquid Glass system: light ground, white panels with hairlines, grayscale with one accent, line icons, glass only where layers overlap.

Logo

Five tally marks: four strokes and the fifth across them. The mark says "counting" without a word, reads at 16 px, and is the same drawing as the app icon: white marks on the accent gradient. No other colour is ever added to it.

Notifications

One reminder per habit, at the minute chosen in the habit's sheet. The title is the habit, the body is the target, the actions are the two things you'd do: mark it done or push it back. Lock screen on the left, in-app banner on the right. Grayscale with the app icon as the only colour.
